# FAOSTAT Colombia – Interactive Dashboard

Interactive dashboard developed using FAOSTAT agricultural production data for Colombia.

The dashboard supports the analysis of historical agricultural production, emerging sectors, innovation opportunities, and potential new business lines.

## Business Questions

The analysis addresses four key questions:

1. **What are the main agricultural products in Colombia?**
   - Identify the products that dominate national agricultural production.

2. **What are the historical production trends?**
   - Analyze how agricultural production has evolved over the last 63 years.

3. **Where are there opportunities for innovation?**
   - Identify high-production products with potential for technological improvement.
   - Detect growing market niches and emerging opportunities.

4. **Which products show potential for new business lines?**
   - Identify growing products with potential for processing, fermentation, and development of value-added products.
   - Identify high-volume sectors with potential for by-product and waste valorization.

## Key Focus Areas

- Historical trend analysis
- Agricultural sector analysis
- Emerging sector identification
- Innovation opportunity analysis
- Data-driven R&D recommendations
- New business opportunity identification
- Interactive data visualization
